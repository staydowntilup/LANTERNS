Kyle Rayner

The only Lantern.

Kyle Rayner was a freelance graphic artist when a dying alien named Ganthet handed him the last power ring in existence. For a time, Kyle was the only Green Lantern protecting the universe, which earned him the nickname "the Torchbearer."

Kyle's imagination sets him apart from every other ring bearer. Most Lanterns build simple shields or blunt weapons, Kyle's artistic mind produces some of the most creative and precise energy constructs ever recorded by the Corps. He often used his ability as a comic to create dragons, superheros, and other living constructs.

Kyle has also carried titles no other Lantern has held, including Ion and White Lantern. As a White Lantern, Kyle was able to access all seven colors of the emotional spectrum, giving him abilities beyond those of a normal Green Lantern. His creativity, determination, and experience have made him one of the most important and powerful members of the Green Lantern Corps.
