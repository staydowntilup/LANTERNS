Why I built this

I've been reading Green Lantern comics on and off for years, and the Corps has always stood out to me for a simple reason: the ring runs on willpower, not luck or birthright. Anyone stubborn enough can wear one, and the stories are really about what a person does with that kind of responsibility. Hal, Guy, and Kyle all answer that question differently, and I wanted a place that put their answers side by side instead of scattered across wikis and old forum posts.

This project started as an assingment way to practice HTML and CSS, but it gave me an excuse to read thirty years of issues "for research." WHile reeading the comics and watching the show/movies the entire DC universe really captivated me and really helped me find an appreciation for the universe. THis side made me fall back in love with different characters I appreciated when I was younger like Nightwing and Robin.

What kept me motivated

Honestly, alot of the small stuff kept me very interested in building the site. Getting stats table to line up correctly and little task like that felt good to see myself complete. Every time I added a new detail, or added something I'd forgotten about (Guy's stint as a Red Lantern, Kyle carrying the entire Corps as its only member), the site got a little more useful, and that kept me coming back to it.
