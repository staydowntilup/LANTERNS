Hal Jordan

Green Lantern of Sector 2814 ; the OG Green Lantern

Hal Jordan was a reckless test pilot when a dying alien named Abin Sur crash landed on Earth and chose him to inherit his power ring. He was the first human Green Lantern, and quickly became one of the most trusted and rule bending members of Corps.

Hal Jordan is one of the most accomplished Green Lanterns in DC and has taken part in some of the biggest battles in the universe. He has fought powerful enemies such as Sinestro, Parallax, and the Anti-Monitor, while also serving as a leader of the Green Lantern Corps. During the Sinestro Corps War, Hal helped lead the Green Lanterns against Sinestro’s army. Hal also played a major role during Blackest Night, where he fought the Black Lanterns and ultimately faced the Black Lantern Spectre.

Despite being kicked out, and nearly wiping out all of the Corps more than once, Hal's always found his way back to the ring. He has made some serious mistakes throughout his career, but also managed to overcome them and earn his place as a Green Lantern again.
