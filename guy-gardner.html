Guy Gardner

Green Lantern of Sector 2814; the Corps' loudest recruit.

Guy Gardner was Abin Sur's backup choice to be a green lantern, but was selected alongside Hal Jordan. He is a former teacher and gym coach with an extremely short temper and an even shorter fuse, Guy spent years feeling like a shadow behind the corps' finest before proving himself as one of its strongest and most loyal members.

Guy's willpower doesn't come from discipline like Hal's; it comes from his stubbornness. He uses his ring to match his fighting style, combining his toughness with his attacks.This makes his ring constructs a little blunt, oversized, and built for maximum impact .

Guy has been a member of the Green Lantern Corps, Justice League, and Justice League International, and has fought alongside heroes such as Superman, Batman, Hal Jordan, and John Stewart. After losing his Green Lantern ring, Guy became known as Warrior and discovered that he had Vuldarian DNA, which gave him the ability to create weapons and change parts of his body. He has also used different types of power rings, including Sinestro's yellow ring and a Red Lantern ring. Guy became a Red Lantern and was able to survive the effects of the ring, something that normally causes extreme rage and can be deadly.

Signature Traits

  • Blunt, oversized constructs (boxing gloves, battering rams)
  • Short temper, but loyal to friends and allies
  • Former U.S. Marine, college football player and gym teacher
  • Nicknamed "the Lantern with the biggest ego" in the Corps
  • Has briefly served as a Red Lantern and Yellow Lantern
